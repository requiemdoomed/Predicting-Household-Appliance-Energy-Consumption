`data/raw/energydata_complete.csv` is redistributed without modification.
`data/example_conditions.csv` is a derived selection of three original rows with target, lighting and random columns removed.
`data/example_history.csv` is a derived selection of 144 original timestamps and energy measurements.

Plots, features and analysis are derived from the source data. The dataset creator does not endorse this project. The MIT software license does not relicense the data.

Associated publication: Candanedo, L. M., Feldheim, V., & Deramaix, D. (2017). Data driven prediction models of energy use of appliances in a low-energy house. Energy and Buildings, 140, 81-97. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2017.01.083
